# Research

Research helps us understand the current state of AI and the evidence behind important advances.

```text
What do we know about a field?
        ↓
Surveys
        ↓
What did a particular study contribute?
        ↓
Papers and reports
```

## Research Areas

- [Surveys](01-Surveys/README.md) — broad overviews of an AI field, its concepts, methods, datasets, and major papers.
- [Papers and Reports](02-Papers-and-Reports/README.md) — important research papers, technical reports, and concise summaries of their contributions and evidence.
